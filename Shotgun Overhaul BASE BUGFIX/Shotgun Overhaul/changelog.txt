/// Shotgunner \\\

The Shotgunner skill tree has been replaced with a new one that focuses more on utility as opposed to damage. This allows shotguns
to be balanced for viability without a huge point investment (though they'll still be better if you decide to).

T1 - Dexterity
-Basic
	-Swap time with shotguns reduced by 33%
-Aced
	-Sprint and reload with shotguns
	
T2 - Clay Shooter
-Basic
	-+8 accuracy with shotguns
-Aced
	-40% to accuracy bonus when aiming shotguns. 100% increased ADS speed with shotguns
	
T2 - Steady Hand
-Basic
	-+8 stability with shotguns
-Aced
	-50% faster reload speed for shotguns
	
T3 - Modified Choke
-Basic
	-50% increased effective range when aiming with shotguns
-Aced
	-Damage falloff penalty reduced by half when aiming with shotguns
	
T3 - Spray and Pray
-Basic
	-Sprint and hipfire with shotguns
-Aced
	-Magazine-fed shotguns double their base magazine size
	
T4 - Rage
-Basic
	-Killing an enemy within 18m with a shotgun causes nearby enemies to panic. Cannot occur more than once every 5 seconds
-Aced
	-Killing an enemy within 18m with a shotgun gives a 20% movement speed bonus for 3 seconds
